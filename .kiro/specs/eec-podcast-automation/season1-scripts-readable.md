## Episode 1 — “The Voice Note”  ·  A2  ·  ~6.4 min

**Situation:** Macal records a fake-success voice note to his mother in Al Nahda, then takes a call from Dune & Co.  
**Arc beat:** We meet Macal, his dream, his fear. He lies by omission.  
**Phonetic focus (Accent Lab):** Word stress — Which syllable carries the beat (e.g. reCORD the verb vs. REcord the noun). Getting stress right matters more than any single sound.  
**Phrase of the episode:** *Can you repeat that, please?* — مُمْكِن تْعِيد دَه تَانِي، لَوْ سَمَحْت؟  
**Cliffhanger:** Caller: we need your two-minute video by Friday. Tarek texts: dont embarrass us.


**— COLD OPEN —**

- **Macal:** Hello, Mama. I am in Dubai. Everything is good. Everything is... very good.  _(earnest, slightly forced cheer)_
- **Macal:** My job is great. The company is big. The money is... yes, it is good. Do not worry.  _(practiced, hollow)_
- **Macal:** Sorry, what? Sorry, can you repeat that, please? I did not understand.  _(panicked, switching tone)_
- **Interviewer:** Dune and Company. We need your answer by Friday. Are you still with us?  _(professional, clipped)_
- **Macal:** Yes. Yes, I am here. Sorry, one moment. I am listening.  _(caught, breath held)_

## Episode 2 — “Taxi to Deira”  ·  A2  ·  ~5.6 min

**Situation:** Late for work, Macal takes a taxi driven by Ravi through Dubai traffic.  
**Arc beat:** Macal must speak to be understood. He meets Nour at Dune & Co.  
**Phonetic focus (Accent Lab):** Flap-T — In American English, T between vowels sounds like a soft D: water -> 'wader', city -> 'ciddy', later -> 'lader'.  
**Phrase of the episode:** *Can you please go faster?* — مُمْكِن لَو سَمَحْت تِمْشِي أَسْرَع؟  
**Cliffhanger:** He arrives late and sees Tarek charming the front desk.


**— COLD OPEN —**

- **Macal:** Please... can you go faster? I am late for work.  _(anxious, rapid, breathless)_
- **Taxi driver:** Traffic is heavy today, my friend. But I know a short way.  _(calm, reassuring, Indian accent)_
- **Macal:** How long... how much time to Deira?  _(urgent, stumbling, earnest)_
- **Taxi driver:** Maybe twenty minutes. My brother, you are safe with me.  _(warm, confident)_
- **Macal:** Okay. Thank you. Please... just please hurry.  _(quiet panic, exhale)_

**— COACH INTRO (Mahmoud) —**

- **MAHMOUD (coach):** أَهْلًا يَا صَاحْبِي! أَنَا مَحْمُود، وَدِي حَلْقَة تَانِي مِن يَلَّا فْلُوئَنْت... النَّهَارْدَة مَكَّال رَاكِب تَاكْسِي، وَمِتْأَخِّر عَن الشُّغْل، وَالسَّوَاقْ رَاجُو بِيَقُول لُه كَلَام كِتِير!  _(warm, energetic welcome, big smile)_
- **MAHMOUD (coach):** اسْمَع كْوَيِّس... فِي الْحَلْقَة دِي، هَنِتْعَلَّم تَلَاتَة عِبَارَات مُهِمَّة جِدًّا: الْأَوَّلَانِي 'How long'... يَعْنِي كَم الْوَقْت.  _(slowing down on the English phrase, clear)_
- **MAHMOUD (coach):** وَالتَّانْيَة 'go faster'... يَعْنِي امْشِي أَسْرَع. وَالتَّالْتَة 'Can you please'... أَدَبْ وَلُطْف، وَدِي أَهَمّ وَاحْدَة!  _(playful emphasis on the last phrase)_
- **MAHMOUD (coach):** يَلَّا بِينَا نِسْمَع مَكَّال وَرَاجُو فِي التَّاكْسِي... وَخُد بِالك، هَتِسْمَع كُلْ عِبَارَة دِي بِالْإِنْجِلِيزِي وَاضِح!  _(excited, inviting, gentle handoff to the scene)_

- **MAHMOUD (coach):** دِلْوَقْتِي الْمُهِمّ... الْأَكْسِنْت لَابْ! النَّهَارْدَة هَنِشْتَغَل عَلَى صَوْت الـ Flap-T. فِي الْأَمْرِيكِي، لَمَّا حَرْف T يِيجِي بَيْن حَرْفِين مُتَحَرِّكِين، بِيْبْقَى زَي دَالْ نَاعْمَة... water بِيْبْقَى wader... city بِيْبْقَى ciddy... later بِيْبْقَى lader.  _(playful, clear articulation, accent lab energy)_

- **Macal:** Ten thirty? So I am not late. Really? I am not late.  _(disbelieving, then lightening)_
