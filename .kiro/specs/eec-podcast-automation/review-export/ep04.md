## Episode 4 — “The Complaint”  ·  B1  ·  ~5.5 min

**Situation:** A client is furious about a double booking at Dune & Co.; Macal must de-escalate.  
**Arc beat:** Macal stays calm and uses English under pressure.  
**Phonetic focus (Accent Lab):** Linking — Native speakers connect words: 'an apartment' -> 'a-napartment', 'pick it up' -> 'pi-ki-tup'. Words flow into each other.  
**Phrase of the episode:** *Just to confirm* — يَعْنِي أَتْأَكَّد بَس  
**Cliffhanger:** The client is Nours boss, who asks Macal to handle the follow-up call.


**— COLD OPEN —**

- **Taxi driver:** Sir, please — she has been waiting outside for twenty minutes. She is very, very angry.  _(nervous, apologetic, fast)_
- **Macal:** Okay. Okay, I'm coming. I mean — I'm on my way down right now.  _(tense, breathing hard, calming himself)_
- **Friend:** You double-booked my suite. I flew fourteen hours for this. Fourteen!  _(furious, sharp, escalating)_
- **Macal:** Ma'am, I'm so sorry. Please — give me one minute, and I will fix this.  _(steady, sincere, low and calm)_

- **MAHMOUD (coach):** النَّهَارْدَه مَعَانَا مَاكَال فِي مَوْقِف صَعْب... كْلَايَنْت زَعْلَانَة جِدًّا، وَهُوَ لَازِم يِهَدِّي الدِّنْيَا بِالإِنْجِلِيزِي.  _(storytelling, building tension)_
- **MAHMOUD (coach):** اِسْمَعُوا كْوَيِّس لِتَلَاتَة تَعْبِيرَات... I'm so sorry، وَ Let me fix this، وَ Just to confirm.  _(clear, pointing out key phrases)_
- **MAHMOUD (coach):** يَلَّا... يِلَا نِتْعَلِّم وَنِتْسَلِّي!  _(energetic, encouraging close)_

**— ACT 1 —**

- **Macal:** Ma'am, I'm so sorry about this. I've been checking your booking since I got the call. Please, come inside — let's sit down and I'll fix this.  _(calm, sincere, slightly nervous)_
- **Friend:** Sit down? I don't want to sit down. I want my room. I booked the Palm Suite three weeks ago, and now your colleague tells me someone else is already inside.  _(angry, voice rising)_
- **Macal:** I understand. You have every right to be upset. Just to confirm — your name is Ms. Hana, and the reservation is for four nights, yes?  _(steady, trying to slow things down)_
- **Friend:** Five nights. Five. And I live here since two years — no, wait, that's not the point. The point is I am tired and I have no room.  _(exhausted, frustrated)_
- **Macal:** You're right, and I'm sorry for the mix-up. Here's what I can do right now. We have a sea-view suite on the eighth floor. It's bigger than the Palm Suite, and I can offer it to you tonight at the same price.  _(confident, solution-focused)_
- **Friend:** The same price? For a bigger suite? That... that sounds too good. Are you sure you can do that?  _(surprised, softening slightly)_
- **Macal:** Yes, I'm sure. And I'll add free breakfast for your whole stay. If that works for you, I'll take you up myself and make sure everything is perfect.  _(warm, reassuring)_
- **Friend:** Okay. Okay, fine. But if the room is not ready, I'm going to be very, very unhappy.  _(still tense, but calming)_
- **Macal:** Deal. Give me two minutes — I'll confirm with the front desk, and then we'll go up together. Thank you for giving me the chance to fix this.  _(grateful, controlled)_
- **Friend:** Two minutes. I'll wait here. And... thank you. Most people here just hand me a key and walk away.  _(quiet, a little surprised by the kindness)_

**— COACH BREAK (Mahmoud) —**

- **MAHMOUD (coach):** طَيِّب، يَا جَمَاعَة... مَاكَال عَمَلْ حَاجَة صَعْبَة جِدًّا: هَدَّى الدِّنْيَا بِالإِنْجِلِيزِي! خُدُوا بَالْكُمْ مِنْ تَلَاتَة تَعْبِيرَات. أَوَّل وَاحِد: I'm so sorry. مَعْنَاهَا آسِف بِجِدّ... بِنِسْتَخْدِمْهَا لَمَّا الغَلْطَة كَبِيرَة، مِشْ زَيِّ I'm sorry العَادِيَّة. مَثَلًا: I'm so sorry about the delay.  _(warm, teaching clearly)_
- **MAHMOUD (coach):** تَانِي تَعْبِير: Let me fix this. يَعْنِي سِيبْنِي أَنَا أَصْلَحْ المَوْقِف. بِنِقُولْهَا لَمَّا الكْلَايَنْت زَعْلَان وِنِحْنَا عَايْزِين نِطَمْنَه. مَثَلًا: Please, give me one minute — let me fix this.  _(reassuring, steady)_

- **MAHMOUD (coach):** وَالغَلْطَة المُشْتَرَكَة... وَلَا يِهِمَّك، كُلِّنَا بِنِعْمِلْهَا. المَصْرِي بِيِقُولْ I live here since two years، لِأَنَّ فِي العَرَبِي بِنِقُولْ "مِن سَنَتَيْن". بَسْ بِالإِنْجِلِيزِي الصَّح: I've lived here for two years.  _(gentle, no shaming)_
- **MAHMOUD (coach):** دِلْوَقْتِي... Accent Lab! النَّهَارْدَه عَنْ اللِّينْكِينْغ. الأَمْرِيكَان بِيِوْصَلُوا الكَلِمَات بَعْض، مِشْ بِيِفْصَلُوهَا. مَثَلًا فِي الجُمْلَة: I'm on my way down... بْتُسْمَعْ كِدَه: I-mo-ny-way-down. جَرِّبْ مَعَايَا: I'm on my way. تَانِي مَرَّة: I'm on my way. بْرَافُو!  _(playful, energetic drill)_

**— ACT 2 —**

- **Macal:** Ms. Hana, your suite is ready. I checked it myself. The view from the eighth floor is honestly better than the Palm Suite.  _(calm, warm, small smile)_
- **Friend:** You checked it yourself? Okay... wow. That's good service. I've lived here for two years, and nobody ever did that for me.  _(surprised, softening)_
- **Macal:** Just to confirm — you'll have breakfast at eight, and I'll call you at seven to make sure everything is fine.  _(professional, reassuring)_
- **Friend:** That works. You know what, young man? You should meet my boss. She runs Rising Voices. She's always looking for people who stay calm when clients shout.  _(impressed, thoughtful)_
- **Macal:** Rising Voices? I mean... that's the fellowship I applied for. Are you serious?  _(stunned, voice cracking slightly)_
- **Friend:** Very serious. I'm calling her now. Her name is Nour's manager — Ms. Layla. She'll want a follow-up call tomorrow at nine. Can you handle that?  _(brisk, businesslike)_
- **Macal:** I can. I've handled worse tonight, right? Just to confirm — tomorrow, nine o'clock. I'll be ready.  _(determined, steady)_
- **Friend:** Good. And Macal — fix that mix-up with the booking system. My boss hates double bookings even more than I do.  _(half-joking, walking away)_
- **Macal:** Noted. Let me fix this one thing first, and then... I have a call to prepare for.  _(to himself, quiet resolve)_
