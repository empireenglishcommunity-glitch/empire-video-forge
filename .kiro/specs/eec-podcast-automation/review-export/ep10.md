## Episode 10 — “Two Worlds”  ·  B2  ·  ~6.1 min

**Situation:** Final interview/offer day at Dune & Co.  
**Arc beat:** Macal negotiates, tells the truth, and chooses his future.  
**Phonetic focus (Accent Lab):** Review + connected speech — Put it all together - stress, flap-T, TH, linking, schwa, intonation - in one flowing, confident, natural American delivery.  
**Phrase of the episode:** *what you're offering* — إِنْتَ بِتَعْرِضْ إِيهْ بِالظَّبْطْ  
**Cliffhanger:** Macal gets the offer; Nour: Im staying if you want me to. Tarek asks to co-build a learning studio. End hook: a new voice message from a learner in Cairo.


**— COLD OPEN —**

- **Interviewer:** So, Macal. We love the portfolio. But tell me — why should we pick you over the other finalists?  _(calm, measured, testing him)_
- **Macal:** Honestly? Because I'm not gonna pretend to be someone else. I know my English isn't perfect, but my work speaks louder than my accent.  _(steady, sincere, quiet confidence)_
- **Interviewer:** Interesting. And if we offered you the role today — what would you say?  _(leaning in, curious)_
- **Macal:** I'd say thank you. Then I'd ask what you're offering — because I've got two worlds counting on this answer.  _(firm, a small smile in his voice)_

**— COACH INTRO (Mahmoud) —**

- **MAHMOUD (coach):** أَهْلًا يَا جَمَاعَة! أَنَا مَحْمُود، وَمَعَاكُم فِي يَالّا فْلُوِنْت...  _(warm, welcoming)_
- **MAHMOUD (coach):** النَّهَارْدَه يَوْمْ كَبِيرْ فِي حَيَاةْ مَكَال... يَوْمْ الإِنْتَرْفْيُو الأَخِيرْ فِي دُونْ أَنْدْ كُو، وَقَرَارْ كَبِيرْ عَلَى المَحَكْ!  _(building excitement)_
- **MAHMOUD (coach):** اسْمَعُوا كُوَيِّسْ لِتَلَاتَة تَعْبِيرَاتْ: "my work speaks louder than my accent"، وَ"what you're offering"، وَ"I intend to"...  _(emphasis, teaching)_
- **MAHMOUD (coach):** دُولْ مِفْتَاحْ كَلَامْ النَّهَارْدَه! يَالّا نِسْمَعْ مَكَالْ بِيِتَفَاوَضْ وَبِيِخْتَارْ مُسْتَقْبَلُه... يَالّا!  _(energetic, playful)_

**— ACT 1 —**

- **Interviewer:** Let's be direct, Macal. Tarek has an MBA and ten years at a top firm. You have three years and a lot of heart. Convince me that's enough.  _(calm, probing, slight Indian accent, measured)_
- **Macal:** I can't compete with his resume, and I'm not gonna try. What I can tell you is this — I live here since two years, and in that time I've shipped every project on that table. Nobody had to teach me twice.  _(confident, steady, slight warmth underneath)_
- **Interviewer:** That's a fair point. But heart doesn't always survive deadlines. What happens when it gets hard?  _(testing, leaning forward, curious)_
- **Macal:** It already got hard. Three months ago I almost lost this chance. I stayed, I learned, I showed up. That's the proof you're asking for — not a diploma, a track record.  _(grounded, honest, not defensive)_
- **Interviewer:** Alright. So if we move forward — what are you looking for, exactly?  _(shifting tone, more open now)_
- **Macal:** Before I answer that, I wanna thank you for this process. It's been honest, and that means something. Now — what you're offering matters more than what I'm asking. So tell me the number, the title, and the growth path. Then I'll tell you if I can say yes.  _(calm, respectful, negotiating with quiet authority)_
- **Interviewer:** Fifteen percent above your current salary. Senior associate. And a mentorship track with the design lead.  _(matter-of-fact, watching for reaction)_
- **Macal:** That's solid. One thing — I intend to keep building Macal Empire on the side. It doesn't compete with Dune, it feeds it. If that's a problem, we should talk about it now, not later.  _(firm but warm, laying cards on the table)_
- **Interviewer:** Honestly? That's the first honest answer I've heard all week. Let me make a call. Give me ten minutes.  _(slight smile, decisive, rising to leave)_
- **Macal:** Take your time. I'll be right here, breathing.  _(light joke, half-laugh, releasing tension)_

**— COACH BREAK (Mahmoud) —**

- **MAHMOUD (coach):** طَيِّبْ... خُدْ بَالَكْ مِنْ تَلَاتَة تَعْبِيرَاتْ!  _(warm, playful, engaged)_
- **MAHMOUD (coach):** أَوَّلْ وَاحِدْ، لَمَّا تِقُولْ: my work speaks louder than my accent... يَعْنِي شُغْلِي بِيِتَكَلَّمْ عَنِّي، أَكْتَرْ مِنْ لَهْجَتِي. تِسْتَعْمِلْهَا لَمَّا حَدْ يِشَكِّكْ فِي قُدْرَتِكْ بِسَبَبْ الإِنْجْلِيزِي. مِثَالْ: I may not sound perfect, but my work speaks louder than my accent.  _(clear, teaching, builds confidence)_
- **MAHMOUD (coach):** تَانِي وَاحِدْ: What you're offering... يَعْنِي إِنْتَ بِتَعْرِضْ إِيهْ بِالظَّبْطْ؟ دِي طَرِيقَةْ مُؤَدَّبَةْ إِنَّكْ تِسْأَلْ عَنْ الرَّقَمْ وَاللَّقَبْ، بَدَلْ مَا تِقُولْ: how much? مِثَالْ: Before I decide, tell me what you're offering.  _(patient, instructive)_
- **MAHMOUD (coach):** تَالِتْ وَاحِدْ: I intend to... يَعْنِي نَاوِي أَعْمَلْ إِيهْ. دِي أَقْوَى مِنْ I want، لِأَنَّهَا بْتُوضِّحْ نِيَّةْ وَخُطَّةْ. مِثَالْ: I intend to keep learning every single day.  _(firm, encouraging)_
- **MAHMOUD (coach):** غَلْطَةْ شَائِعَةْ: مَكَالْ قَالْ: I live here since two years. لِيهْ؟ لِأَنَّ فِي العَرَبِي بِنْقُولْ: أَنَا عَايِشْ هِنَا مِنْ سَنِتَيْن، فَبِنْقَلِّدْهَا زِي مَا هِي. الصَّحْ: I've lived here for two years. الـ since مَعَ نُقْطَةْ بِدَايَةْ... وَالـ for مَعَ مُدَّةْ زَمَنْ.  _(gentle, no shaming, corrective)_
- **MAHMOUD (coach):** يَالّا بَقَى مَعَ الـ Accent Lab! خُدْ كَلْمَةْ track record... الإِنْجْلِيزِي بِيِقُولْهَا: track record، وَالـ T وَالـ R مَلْزُوقِينْ. مِشْ track... record. كَرَّرْ وَرَايَا: track record.  _(energetic, playful drill)_
- **MAHMOUD (coach):** وَكَلْمَةْ تَانْيَةْ: what you're offering... لَمَّا بْتِتْكَلَّمْ بِسُرْعَةْ، الـ t بْتِبْقَى زِي d خَفِيفَةْ: what you're offering... رَبْطْ نَاعِمْ، مِشْ what you are offering. كَرِّرْ: what you're offering. عَاشْ! كِدَا بِقِيتْ أَمْرِيكِي!  _(warm, celebratory, drill)_

**— ACT 2 —**

- **Interviewer:** Alright, Macal. I made the call. The answer is yes — senior associate, fifteen percent, and the mentorship track. But I have one condition.  _(brisk, warm, businesslike)_
- **Macal:** Name it. I've lived here for two years now, so I've learned to listen before I react.  _(calm, confident, slight smile)_
- **Interviewer:** Keep Macal Empire alive. Honestly, that side project is the reason I'm signing you. Just don't let it eat your mornings.  _(dry, sincere)_
- **Macal:** Deal. And thank you — for being straight with me from the first question. That's rarer than a good salary.  _(grateful, steady)_
- **Nour:** So? Don't make me guess — I've been standing in that hallway pretending to read a menu for twenty minutes.  _(bright, teasing, excited)_
- **Macal:** I got it. Senior associate. And listen — I intend to keep building my studio, and they actually want that.  _(proud, a little breathless)_
- **Nour:** Macal. That's huge. I'm staying in Dubai — if you want me to. I mean, for the studio. If you're building something real.  _(soft, sincere, slightly nervous)_
- **Tarek:** Congratulations. I mean it. Look — I've been thinking. What if we co-build a learning studio? Your voice, my structure. Two worlds, one table.  _(measured, surprisingly open)_
- **Macal:** Two worlds, one table. I like that. But first — I need a minute. My phone's been buzzing all morning.  _(curious, guarded)_
- **Friend:** Macal? I'm calling from Cairo. A learner heard your story on the podcast and she wants to know — can she really start from zero?  _(urgent, hopeful, distant phone line)_

**— COACH OUTRO (Mahmoud) —**

- **MAHMOUD (coach):** وَابْعَتَهَا عَلَى تِيلِيجْرَامْ فِي مَجْمُوعَةْ Empire English Community... إِحْنَا مِسْتَحْكِمِينْ، بِنِسْمَعْ لَهْجَتَكْ، وَنِشَجَّعَكْ!  _(warm, welcoming)_
- **MAHMOUD (coach):** مِتْخَافْشْ مِنْ الْأَكْسِنْتْ... إِنْتَ مِشْ بِتِتْحَنِّمْ، إِنْتَ بِتِتْكَلَّمْ. يَالّا وَرَّنَا صَوْتَكْ، وَنِشُوفْ حَلْقَةْ الْجَايْ!  _(cheerful, rallying)_
