## Episode 8 — “The Mock”  ·  B2-  ·  ~5.7 min

**Situation:** Tarek and Macal practice interviews.  
**Arc beat:** Rivalry cracks into honesty; Macals English sharpens.  
**Phonetic focus (Accent Lab):** Sentence stress — Stress the content words (nouns, verbs, adjectives) and swallow the function words (the, of, to, a). This gives English its rhythm.  
**Phrase of the episode:** *Tell me about a time* — حِكِي لِي عَنْ مَرَّة  
**Cliffhanger:** Tarek admits his father is sick; Macals mother announces she is visiting Dubai.


**— COLD OPEN —**

- **Tarek:** Stop. You're rambling again. Tell me about a time you solved a problem.  _(sharp, cutting him off)_
- **Macal:** Okay, okay. So at Dune and Co., we were gonna miss a big deadline, right?  _(confident, quick recovery)_
- **Tarek:** Better. But what was YOUR role in that? Be specific.  _(probing, testing him)_
- **Macal:** I led it. I broke the task down, and we delivered two days early.  _(steady, proud)_
- **Tarek:** Hm. Honestly? That was strong.  _(quiet, genuine respect)_

**— COACH INTRO (Mahmoud) —**

- **MAHMOUD (coach):** أَهْلًا يَا صَحْبِي!... أَنَا مَحْمُود، وَمَعَاك فِي يَلَّا فْلُوئِنْت. النَّهَارْدَه مَعَانَا مُقَابَلَة وِهْمِيَّة، مَكَّال وَطَارِق بِيتْدَرَّبُوا... وَالدَّرْس كُلُّه عَنْ إِزَّاي تْرُدّ مِن غِير مَا تْتْلَخْبِط.  _(warm, energetic welcome, one breath)_
- **MAHMOUD (coach):** خَلِّي وَدْنَك مَعَايَا... وَاسْمَع تَلَاتَة تَعْبِيرَات: Tell me about a time، أَيْ حِكِي لِي عَنْ مَرَّة... وَ STAR method، يَعْنِي تِرْتِب قِصَّتَك... وَآخِر وَاحْدَة: Could you clarify، لَمَّا تِحْتَاج تِسْأَل تَوْضِيح.  _(playful, slowly naming the phrases)_
- **MAHMOUD (coach):** يَلَّا... نِسْمَع مَكَّال بِيِتْحَسَّن شُوَيَّة شُوَيَّة. رَكِّز مَعَايَا، وَدُوس بْلَاي!  _(encouraging, upbeat close)_

**— ACT 1 —**

- **Tarek:** Alright, reset. This time I'll be the interviewer from Rising Voices. No mercy.  _(competitive, half-joking)_
- **Macal:** No mercy? Fine. Hit me. I've been prepping all week — I even record myself at night.  _(confident, teasing back)_
- **Tarek:** Good. So — tell me about a time you had to handle a difficult teammate.  _(clipped, interviewer tone)_
- **Macal:** Sure. So last quarter, two people on my team weren't speaking to each other. The work was stalling. I set up a short call, just the three of us. I asked each one what they needed. Turns out it was a misunderstanding about roles. We fixed it in one meeting, and the project moved again. My role? I was the one who pushed to talk it out early.  _(measured, structured, STAR rhythm)_
- **Tarek:** Okay. That's clean. Situation, task, action, result. You actually used the STAR method without sounding like a robot.  _(grudging respect)_
- **Macal:** I live here since two years — so I've had practice, believe me.  _(relaxed, slight laugh — the small slip goes unnoticed by him)_
- **Tarek:** Good. Now I'm gonna throw you a curve. What's your biggest weakness?  _(sharp, testing)_
- **Macal:** Hm. Honestly? I used to say yes to everything. I thought it made me useful. Then I missed a deadline because I took on too much. Now I ask more questions before I commit.  _(honest, reflective)_
- **Tarek:** Wait — before you answer something like that, you should ask what they mean. 'Could you clarify what kind of weakness?' It buys you a second and shows you're careful.  _(instructive, leaning in)_
- **Macal:** Right. Could you clarify... okay, I'll use that next time. That's smart.  _(lightbulb moment, genuine)_
- **Tarek:** I'm not saying this to be nice, but — you've gotten better. Like, actually better.  _(quiet, sincere, almost reluctant)_
- **Macal:** That means a lot, coming from you. Seriously. Let's do one more — I wanna nail this.  _(warm, determined)_

**— COACH BREAK (Mahmoud) —**

- **MAHMOUD (coach):** يَلَّا يَا صَحْبِي، نِفَكِّك سَوَا... أَوَّل تَعْبِير: Tell me about a time. يَعْنِي حِكِي لِي عَنْ مَرَّة، وِدِي أَكْتَر سُؤَال بِيتْكَرَّر فِي المُقَابَلَات. بِيِسْتَخْدِمُوه عَشَان يِشُوفُوا إِنْتَ عَمَلْت إِيه بِالظَّبْط، مِش إِنْتَ مِين. مَثَلًا: Tell me about a time you solved a problem.  _(warm, clear teacher energy)_
- **MAHMOUD (coach):** تَانِي تَعْبِير... الـ STAR method. يَعْنِي تِرْتِب قِصَّتَك: Situation، وَبَعْدِين Task، وَبَعْدِين Action، وَآخِر حَاجَة Result. مَكَّال عَمَلْهَا بْرَاحَة فِي المَشْهَد، وِعَشَان كِدَه طَلَع كَلَامُه مُرَتَّب مِن غِير مَا يْبَان رُوبُوت.  _(explaining, rhythmic)_
- **MAHMOUD (coach):** وِتَالِت تَعْبِير: Could you clarify? يَعْنِي تِسْأَل تَوْضِيح. لَمَّا السُّؤَال يْكُون غَامِض، مَتْرُدّش عَلَطُول... بِسْأَل: Could you clarify what you mean? كِدَه بِيكْسَبك ثَانْيَة، وَبْيُورِّي إِنَّك دَقِيق.  _(playful, encouraging)_
- **MAHMOUD (coach):** أَمَّا الغَلْطَة... مَكَّال قَال: I live here since two years. دِي جَايَة مِن العَرَبِي عَشَان إِحْنَا بْنِقُول: أَنَا عَايِش هِنَا مِن سَنَتِين. بِس فِي الإِنْجِلِيزِي، لَمَّا تِتْكَلِّم عَن حَاجَة بَدْأَت فِي المَاضِي وِلِسَّه مُسْتَمِرَّة، بْنِسْتَخْدِم: I've lived here for two years. خَلِّي بَالَك: for، مِش since، مَعَ المُدَّة.  _(gentle, corrective but warm)_
- **MAHMOUD (coach):** وَدِلْوَقْتِي... الـ Accent Lab! النَّهَارْدَه عَنْ Sentence stress — يَعْنِي فِي الإِنْجِلِيزِي، بِنْضَغَط عَلَى الكَلِمَات المُهِمَّة: الأَسْمَاء، الأَفْعَال، الصِّفَات... وِبْنِبْلَع الكَلِمَات الصَّغِيرَة زَي the، of، to، a.  _(energetic, signature segment)_
- **MAHMOUD (coach):** هَات جُمْلَة مِن المَشْهَد: I was the one who pushed to talk it out early. المُصْرِي بِيقُول كُل كِلْمَة بِنَفْس القُوَّة... I-was-the-one-who-pushed. غَلَط! الأَمْرِيكِي بِيعْمِل: I was the ONE who PUSHED to TALK it OUT EARly. جَرِّب مَعَايَا... دُوس عَلَى ONE، PUSHED، TALK، OUT، EARly. كَرِّر بَعْدِي!  _(playful, drilling, call-and-response)_
- **MAHMOUD (coach):** بْرَافُو يَا بَطَل! حِسِّت الفَرْق؟ كِل مَرَّة تِرْكِّز عَلَى الوَقْت، لُغَتُك تِطْلَع أَكْتَر طَبِيعِيَّة. يَلَّا نِرْجَع لِلْقِصَّة... مَكَّال وَطَارِق لِسَّه فِي التَّدْرِيب!  _(warm, celebratory, transition back)_

**— ACT 2 —**

- **Tarek:** One more. And this time, you ask the questions too. An interview goes both ways.  _(challenging, testing him)_
- **Macal:** Deal. So — tell me about a time your team disagreed with your plan. And, could you clarify what you mean by 'disagreed'?  _(confident, slight grin, sharp delivery)_
- **Tarek:** Look at you. You turned my own trick around.  _(caught off guard, genuinely impressed)_
- **Macal:** I've lived here for two years, habibi. I learn fast.  _(playful, light, proud)_
- **Tarek:** Ha. Yeah. You do.  _(laughs, then it fades; a long pause, the smile leaving his face, a slow breath before he lets his guard down)_
- **Tarek:** That was — actually clean. Okay. Honestly? I've been hard on you because I was scared. You're catching up. Fast.  _(quiet now, the joking gone; honest and a little vulnerable)_
- **Macal:** Scared of what?  _(curious, softer now)_
- **Tarek:** My father's sick back in Cairo. I might need to go. And if I leave, you're the one they'll pick for the fellowship.  _(heavy, admitting the truth)_
- **Macal:** Tarek... I didn't know. That's not a race for me. If you need anything, I'm here.  _(warm, sincere, no ego)_
- **Tarek:** Thanks. I'll remember that.  _(grateful, low)_

**— COACH OUTRO (Mahmoud) —**

- **MAHMOUD (coach):** يَلَّا يَا بَطَل، نِلَخَّص بِسُرْعَة... النَّهَارْدَه خَدْنَا تَلَات تَعْبِيرَات: Tell me about a time، عَشَان تِحْكِي قِصَّة مُرَتَّبَة... والـ STAR method، وَهِيَ Situation وَ Task وَ Action وَ Result... وَ Could you clarify، عَشَان تِسْأَل تَوْضِيح بِلُطْف!  _(warm, quick recap, proud energy)_
- **MAHMOUD (coach):** وَعِبَارَة الحَلْقَة... هِيَ: Tell me about a time. دِي أَكْتَر جُمْلَة هَتْسَمْعَهَا فِي المُقَابَلَة، فَخَلِّي صَوْتُك حَاضِر، وَبِدَايَة قِصَّتِك وَاضْحَة.  _(slow, emphatic on the English phrase, inviting)_
- **MAHMOUD (coach):** وَدِلْوَقْتِي، التَّحَدِّي... سَجِّل رِسَالَة صَوْتِيَّة قَصِيرَة، قُول فِيهَا Tell me about a time، وَرَكِّز عَلَى النَّبْر عَلَى TIME، وَابْعَتَهَا لِنَا عَلَى تِلِيجْرَام فِي مَجْمُوعَة Empire English Community.  _(energetic challenge, clear and warm)_
- **MAHMOUD (coach):** إِحْنَا هَنِسْمَع صَوْتُك، وَنِشَجِّعُك... وَمَتْخَافِش مِن الغَلَط، كِدَه بِنِتْعَلِّم! يَلَّا، مُسْتَنِّيك بُكْرَة فِي الحَلْقَة الجَايَة... سَلَام!  _(encouraging, big brother warmth, ending bright)_
