## Episode 1 — “The Voice Note”  ·  A2  ·  ~6.7 min

**Situation:** Macal records a fake-success voice note to his mother in Al Nahda, then takes a call from Dune & Co.  
**Arc beat:** We meet Macal, his dream, his fear. He lies by omission.  
**Phonetic focus (Accent Lab):** Word stress — Which syllable carries the beat (e.g. reCORD the verb vs. REcord the noun). Getting stress right matters more than any single sound.  
**Phrase of the episode:** *Can you repeat that, please?* — مُمْكِن تْعِيد دَه تَانِي، لَوْ سَمَحْت؟  
**Cliffhanger:** Caller: we need your two-minute video by Friday. Tarek texts: dont embarrass us.


**— COLD OPEN —**

- **Macal:** Hello, Mama. I am in Dubai. Everything is good. Everything is... very good.  _(earnest, slightly forced cheer)_
- **Macal:** My job is great. The company is big. The money is... yes, it is good. Do not worry.  _(practiced, hollow)_
- **Macal:** Sorry, what? Sorry, can you repeat that, please? I did not understand.  _(panicked, switching tone)_
- **Interviewer:** Dune and Company. We need your answer by Friday. Are you still with us?  _(professional, clipped)_
- **Macal:** Yes. Yes, I am here. Sorry, one moment. I am listening.  _(caught, breath held)_

**— COACH INTRO (Mahmoud) —**

- **MAHMOUD (coach):** أَهْلًا بِيكُمْ يَا جَمَاعَة فِي يَلَّا فْلُوَنْت! أَنَا مَحْمُود، وَمَعَاكُمْ كُلْ يَوْم، عَشَان نِتْعَلَّم إِنْجِلِيزِي وِإِحْنَا مُبْسُوطِين... النَّهَارْدَة، مَعَانَا مَكَال، مِصْرِي فِي دُبَاي، بِيِحْكِي لِأُمُّه كَلَام كِبِير، وِقَلْبُه مِشْ مِرْتَاح خَالِص.  _(warm, welcoming, a little playful)_
- **MAHMOUD (coach):** وَبَسْ عَشَان نِبْقَى مُتْفِهْمِين مِنْ أَوِّل يَوْم... يَلَّا فْلُوَنْت دَه اسْم البَرْنَامِج اللِّي بِتِسْمَعُوه دَه. أَمَّا البَيْت بِتَاعْنَا كُلِّنَا، العِيلَة اللِّي هَنِتْمَرَّن فِيهَا سَوَا وِنِبْعَت لِبَعْض، دَه عَلَى تِلِيجْرَام فِي Empire English Community. يَلَّا فْلُوَنْت هُوَ الحِكَايَة، وَ Empire English Community هُوَ البَيْت.  _(friendly, clarifying, matter-of-fact)_
- **MAHMOUD (coach):** وَبَعْدِين... التِّلِيفُون بِيِرِن، وَالشُّغْل بِيِسْأَلُه سُؤَال صَعْب. يَا تُرَى هَيِقُول إِيه؟ خَلِّيكُمْ مَعَانَا، وِاسْمَعُوا كْوَيِّس.  _(building suspense, gentle energy)_
- **MAHMOUD (coach):** رَكِّزُوا النَّهَارْدَة عَلَى تَلَاتَة عِبَارَات... لَمَّا تِقَابِل حَد، قُول: Hello, I am... وَتَانِي وَاحِدَة: Nice to meet you. وَلَو مِفْهِمْتِش، قُول بِهُدُوء: Sorry, can you repeat that, please?  _(clear, teaching slowly, encouraging)_
- **MAHMOUD (coach):** يَلَّا بَيْنَا... نِسْمَع مَكَال، وِنِتْعَلَّم مِنُّه!  _(bright, launching into the story)_

**— ACT 1 —**

- **Macal:** No, Mama, I am not tired. Dubai is very nice. The weather is hot, but I am okay.  _(warm but forcing it, voice note energy)_
- **Macal:** My manager? He is very happy with me. Yes. Everything is fine. I live here since two years now, Mama, and—  _(earnest, rushing to reassure)_
- **Macal:** Sorry, Mama, one moment. Sorry, one moment. There is another call. I will call you later. Bye.  _(sudden switch, slightly panicked)_
- **Macal:** Hello? This is Macal speaking.  _(clear, professional, small nervous pause)_
- **Interviewer:** Hello, Macal. This is Mrs. Fernandes from Dune and Company. Am I speaking with the right person?  _(polite, brisk, clipped professional)_
- **Macal:** Yes, yes. I am Macal. Nice to meet you... I mean, nice to speak with you.  _(flustered, correcting himself)_
- **Interviewer:** Nice to meet you too. So, Macal, we have your Rising Voices application here. We need your answer by Friday. Are you still with us?  _(warm but testing, direct)_
- **Macal:** Sorry, can you repeat that, please? I did not understand the last part.  _(quiet, honest, not ashamed)_
- **Interviewer:** Of course. I said, are you still interested in the fellowship? Friday is the deadline.  _(patient, clear, slower)_
- **Macal:** Yes. Yes, I am interested. Very interested. I am working on my project now. Macal Empire. It is... it is coming.  _(voice cracking slightly, covering doubt)_
- **Interviewer:** Good. Send us a short voice note about your idea. Two minutes, no more. Can you do that by Friday?  _(kind, businesslike)_
- **Macal:** Yes, of course. Two minutes. Friday. I understand. Thank you, Mrs. Fernandes.  _(determined, hanging up, exhaling)_
- **Nour:** Macal? You look like you just saw a ghost. What happened?  _(light, teasing, catching him off guard)_
- **Macal:** Nour. Hi. Hello. I am fine. I just... I have a voice note to make. A big one.  _(scattered, switching gears fast)_
- **Nour:** A voice note? For your mom again?  _(amused, curious)_
- **Macal:** No. For Dune and Company. The fellowship. They want my idea in two minutes. My real idea.  _(serious now, eyes wide)_
- **Nour:** Okay, then. Let us hear it. Say it to me first. Right now.  _(encouraging, planting her feet)_
- **Macal:** Now? Here? Nour, I do not know. My English is... okay, but this is important.  _(hesitant, vulnerable)_
- **Nour:** Macal. Your English is good. Just talk. Start with hello. Hello, my name is Macal, and I have an idea.  _(coaching-casual, sincere)_
- **Macal:** Hello. My name is Macal. I am from Egypt. I live in Dubai... and I have an idea. I want to help people learn English. Like me.  _(slow, finding his footing, then warming)_

**— COACH BREAK (Mahmoud) —**

- **MAHMOUD (coach):** يَلّا يَا جَمَاعَة، وِقْفَة سَرِيعَة مَعَ مَحْمُود!... أَوَّل جُمْلَة: Nice to speak with you. يَعْنِي تَشَرَّفْت بِالكَلَام مَعَاك، بِنِقُولْهَا فِي التِّلِيفُون أَكْتَر مِن الوِيش تو وِيش. مَثَلًا: Hello, nice to speak with you, Mrs. Fernandes. تَانِي جُمْلَة: Can you repeat that, please?... دِي أَهَم جُمْلَة لَمَّا مِتْفَهْمِش، وبِتَدِيك فُرْصَة تِسْمَع تَانِي مِن غِير حَرَج. وَتَلَاتَة: Are you still interested? يَعْنِي لِسَّه مُهْتَم؟  _(warm, energetic, teaching)_
- **MAHMOUD (coach):** بَس خُد بِالك: مَاكَال قَالَ Nice to meet you فِي التِّلِيفُون. الغَلْطَة دِي طَبِيعِيَّة... لِأَنَّ فِي العَرَبِي كِلْمَة وَاحْدَة 'تَشَرَّفْنَا' لِلْحَاجَتَيْن. فِي الإِنْجِلِيزِي، فِي المُقَابَلَة الوِيش تو وِيش بِتَقُول Nice to meet you، لَكِن فِي التِّلِيفُون بِتَقُول Nice to speak with you.  _(reassuring, no shame)_
- **MAHMOUD (coach):** دِلْوَقْتِي، لَاب الوَاكْسِنْت!... النَّهَارْدَة عَن Word stress، يَعْنِي إِنْتَه مَبْتِقُولْش الكَلْمَة كِلْهَا بِنَفْس القُوَّة. اسْمَع: application. غَلَط: appliCAtion. صَح: appliCAtion. رَدِّدْ: application. وَكَمَان: interested. غَلَط: interesTED. صَح: INterested. رَدِّدْ: interested. الشِّدَّة بِتَفَرَّق أَكْتَر مِن أَي صَوْت. يَلّا، رَدِّدْ مَعَايَا!  _(playful, drill energy, clear)_

**— ACT 2 —**

- **Nour:** That was good, Macal. Really good. Now send it. Do not think too much.  _(warm, certain)_
- **Macal:** Wait. I am not sure about the ending. It is... it is too simple. And the beginning... I said nice to meet you. On a voice note. That is wrong, yes?  _(anxious, self-checking)_
- **Nour:** You are right. It is nice to speak with you. But Macal, listen. One small word is not your whole idea. Your idea is strong.  _(gentle correction, encouraging)_
- **Macal:** Okay. Okay. I understand. I will fix it. ... Nour, thank you. Really.  _(steadying, grateful)_
- **Nour:** Do not thank me yet. Thank me when they say yes. Go. Record it.  _(playful, pushing him)_
- **Macal:** Hello. My name is Macal. Nice to speak with you, Mrs. Fernandes. I am from Egypt. I live in Dubai, and I have an idea. I want to help people learn English. Like me. This is Macal Empire.  _(clear, proud, deliberate pacing)_
- **Official:** Mr. Macal? This is Dune and Company. Thank you for your voice note. It was good. But we need one more thing.  _(professional, brisk)_
- **Macal:** Sorry, can you repeat that, please? I did not understand the last part.  _(polite, calm)_
- **Official:** Yes, of course. We need your two-minute video by Friday. Not a voice note. A video. Do you understand?  _(firm, clear)_
- **Macal:** Yes. Yes, I understand. A video. Friday. I can do that. Thank you. Nice to speak with you. Goodbye.  _(shocked but composed)_
- **Friend:** Bro, that was Tarek. He says the team is watching. Do not embarrass us.  _(tense, whispered)_

**— COACH OUTRO (Mahmoud) —**

- **MAHMOUD (coach):** يَلّا يَا جَمَاعَة، نِلَخَّص بِسُرْعَة... فِي التِّلِيفُون بِتَقُول Nice to speak with you، وَمِش Nice to meet you. وَلَمَّا مِتْفَهْمِش، بِتُقُول بِهُدُوء: Can you repeat that, please?  _(warm, quick recap, clear emphasis on the two English phrases)_
- **MAHMOUD (coach):** وَدِلْوَقْتِي... عِبَارَة الحَلْقَة! اسْمَع كْوَيِّس: Can you repeat that, please? دِي الْعِبَارَة اللي هَتْفَتْح لَك كُل الأَبْوَاب، لِأَنَّهَا بِتَقُول لِلنَّاس: أَنَا مُحْتَرِم، وَأَنَا بِتَعَلَّم، وَكَمَّل مَعَايَا!  _(proud, building energy, then soft and encouraging)_
- **MAHMOUD (coach):** فَخُد الوَاجِب دَه... سَجِّل ڤُويس نُوت قَصِير، عَشَر ثَوَانِي بَس، وَقُول فِيهَا: Hello, nice to speak with you. Can you repeat that, please? بِصَوْت وَاضِح، وَبِالثِّقَة، زَي مَا اتَّفَقْنَا.  _(inviting, playful challenge, slow on the English phrase)_
- **MAHMOUD (coach):** وَابْعَتَهَا عَلَى تِلِيجْرَام... فِي Empire English Community. إِحْنَا هِنِسْمَع صَوْتَك، وَنِشَجَّعَك، وَنِقُول لَك إِيه اللي حِلْو وَإِيه اللي يِتْحَسَّن. إِنْتَ مِش لَوْحْدَك فِي الحِكَايَة دِي!  _(belonging, warm group feeling, slight smile in the voice)_
- **MAHMOUD (coach):** يَلّا... المَايك مَعَاك. رُوح سَجِّل، وَأَنَا مُسْتَني سَمَاع صَوْتَك. وَلَاقِينَا فِي الحَلْقَة الجَايَة، وَمَاكَال... لِسَّه عِنْدُه مُشْكِلَة كَبِيرَة!  _(energetic send-off, teasing hook for next episode)_
