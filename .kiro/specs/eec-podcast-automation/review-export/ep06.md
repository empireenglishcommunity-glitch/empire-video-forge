## Episode 6 — “The Room”  ·  B1+  ·  ~5.8 min

**Situation:** Networking event at Dubai Chamber.  
**Arc beat:** Macal joins conversations and pitches an idea; Tarek takes credit.  
**Phonetic focus (Accent Lab):** /ŋ/ ending — The '-ing' ending is one nasal sound made at the back: 'walking', not 'walkin' and not 'walkeen'. Don't add a hard G or an extra vowel.  
**Phrase of the episode:** *I'll follow up myself* — هَتْكَلِّم أَنا بِنَفْسِي، مُش حَدْ تانِي هَيِكَلِّم عَنِّي  
**Cliffhanger:** Nours boss invites Macal to pitch at Rising Voices.


**— COLD OPEN —**

- **Macal:** Tarek, wait — that pitch about the delivery app? That was mine. I mean, I wrote that last week.  _(tense, controlled anger)_
- **Tarek:** Relax, Macal. It's a networking event. Ideas float around. Nobody owns a conversation.  _(smooth, dismissive)_
- **Nour:** He just told the whole room it was his idea. And the investor believed him.  _(urgent, protective whisper)_
- **Macal:** If I don't say something right now, I've already lost. I'm going in.  _(determined, low breath)_

**— COACH INTRO (Mahmoud) —**

- **MAHMOUD (coach):** أَهْلًا بِيكُم يا جَماعَة فِي يَلّا فْلُوَنْت! أَنا مَحْمُود، وَالنَّهارْدَه مَعانا مَشْهَد مِنْ حَياة ماكال فِي دُبَاي... اْجْتِماع نِتْوَرْكِينْج، وَالْكَلِمْ ماشْي بِالسِّرْعَة!  _(warm, welcoming, energetic)_
- **MAHMOUD (coach):** ماكال عايِز يِدْخُل جُوَّه الكلامْ... ويِقُول رَأْيُه مِنْ غِير ما يِتْلَخْبَط. وَبِسْ... تارِك سَبَقُه وَخَدْ الفِكْرَة لِحْسابُه!  _(suspenseful, building tension)_
- **MAHMOUD (coach):** النَّهارْدَه هِنْسْمَع تَلاتَه عِباراتْ حِلْوِين: عِبارَة تِفْتَح بِها الكَلامْ، وَعِبارَة تِدْخُل بِها عَلى جَماعَة بِتِتْكَلِّم... وَعِبارَة تِلْحَق بِها بَعْدِين، يَعْنِي الفُولُو أَبْ.  _(clear, teaching, cheerful)_
- **MAHMOUD (coach):** يَلّا... سَمِّع كوَيِّس، وَخُد بِالكْ مِنْ كِلْ مِثالْ!  _(motivating, punchy)_

**— ACT 1 —**

- **Macal:** Excuse me — sorry to cut in. I couldn't help overhearing. You were talking about the delivery app for small shops, right?  _(nervous but determined, polite)_
- **Interviewer:** Yes, that's right. Tarek was just telling us how it works. Are you in this space too?  _(curious, measured, Indian-accented but clear)_
- **Macal:** Actually, I've been working on something similar for months. The idea is to help shops that don't have their own app yet. I mean... it's not just delivery. It's a full system.  _(finding his footing, then gaining momentum)_
- **Tarek:** Interesting. Macal and I work at the same company. We bounce ideas off each other all the time. Right, Macal?  _(smooth, territorial, smiling but cold)_
- **Macal:** Sure. But this one came from my notebook, not a chat. I can walk you through the numbers if you have five minutes.  _(calm, firm, holding his ground)_
- **Interviewer:** Five minutes? I like the confidence. Go ahead, walk me through it.  _(amused, genuinely interested)_
- **Nour:** There you are! I've been looking everywhere. Oh — you're already deep in it. Good.  _(warm, quick, supportive, slipping into the group easily)_
- **Macal:** Nour, this is — sorry, I didn't catch your name. We were just getting into the details.  _(light, natural, covering the social gap)_
- **Interviewer:** Faisal. Faisal from Gulf Ventures. And honestly, Macal — you should send me a follow-up this week. Let's talk properly.  _(businesslike, offering the door)_
- **Tarek:** Great. I'll make sure he follows up. We can come together — one team, one pitch.  _(smooth, hijacking again, too eager)_
- **Macal:** I'll follow up myself, Faisal. I live here since two years, this city is my home now. I won't let this idea stay in a notebook.  _(assertive, planting his flag, slight emotional crack)_

**— COACH BREAK (Mahmoud) —**

- **MAHMOUD (coach):** طَيِّب، خُد بِالكْ... ماكال قال: "I live here since two years." دِي غَلْطَة صَغِيرَة، وَبِتْحْصَل عَشان فِي العَرَبِي بِنْقُول "أَنا عايِش هِنا مِنْ سَنِتَيْن"... فَالعَقِل بِيْحَوِّلْها حَرْفِي. بَسْ الإِنْجِلِيزِي بِتَحِبْ present perfect مَعَ since... فَالأَصَح: "I've lived here for two years."  _(warm, explaining, not shaming)_
- **MAHMOUD (coach):** أُول عِبارَة، عِبارَة تِفْتَح بِها الكَلامْ: "I couldn't help overhearing." يَعْنِي "مَعْلِش، سِمِعْت نُصّ الكَلامْ وَما قْدِرْتِش أَمْنَع نَفْسِي." بِتْقُولَها لَمّا تِيجِي تِدْخُل عَلى جَماعَة... زَيّ: "I couldn't help overhearing — is this about the delivery app?"  _(clear, teaching, friendly)_
- **MAHMOUD (coach):** تَانِي عِبارَة، عِبارَة تِدْخُل بِها عَلى جَماعَة بِتِتْكَلِّم: "Sorry to cut in." يَعْنِي أَسْفَه إِنِّي قَطَعْت الكَلامْ... بَسْ بِتَقُولَها بِأَدَبْ وَثِقَة. مِثال: "Sorry to cut in — but I've been working on this for months."  _(energetic, showing the move)_
- **MAHMOUD (coach):** تالِت عِبارَة، الفُولُو أَبْ: "I'll follow up myself." يَعْنِي هَتْكَلِّم أَنا بِنَفْسِي، مُش حَدْ تانِي هَيِكَلِّم عَنِّي. دِي قَوِيَّة جِدًّا فِي الشُّغِل... بَتْوَرِّي إِنَّك صاحِب الفِكْرَة.  _(confident, emphasizing ownership)_
- **MAHMOUD (coach):** وَدِلْوَأْتِي... الأَكْسَنْت لاب! النَّهارْدَه عَلى نُطْق /ŋ/... يَعْنِي آخِر الكَلْمَة اللي بِتْخَلِّص بـ -ing. ما تْقُولِش "walkin"... وَما تْقُولِش "walkeen"... الصَّوْت مِنْ وَرَا اللِّسَانْ، مَرَّة وَاحْدَة. جَرِّب: "talking"... "working"... "walking." كَرَّر وَرَايَا!  _(playful drill, slow and clear)_
- **MAHMOUD (coach):** شَاطِر! اسْمَع الفَرْق... "talkin" — غَلَط. "talking" — صَح. مَرَّة تانِي: "I've been working on something similar." يَلّا، كَرِّر الجُمْلَة كامْلة، وَوَرِّيني!  _(encouraging, proud of listener)_

**— ACT 2 —**

- **Interviewer:** Alright, Macal. I've seen a lot of pitches. Yours has something — a real problem, a real system. Send me the follow-up, and I'll read it properly.  _(impressed, measured)_
- **Macal:** Thank you, Faisal. I've lived here for two years, and I've seen these shops struggle every day. This isn't just an idea for me — it's my neighborhood.  _(warm, steady, proud)_
- **Nour:** That was smooth. You didn't freeze, you didn't let Tarek take the wheel. I'm actually impressed.  _(teasing, genuinely pleased)_
- **Macal:** Honestly? My heart was going like a drum. But I couldn't help overhearing him, and I just... I had to say something.  _(laughing softly, relieved)_
- **Tarek:** Nice work, Macal. Really. But Faisal meets a lot of people. Don't get your hopes too high — it's a long road.  _(polite, thin smile, edge underneath)_
- **Macal:** Maybe. But I'll follow up myself. That part isn't up to anyone else.  _(calm, firm, not rising to it)_
- **Nour:** Macal — hold on. My boss just texted me. She's here tonight, and she wants to meet the guy with the delivery idea.  _(urgent, excited, hushed)_
- **Macal:** Wait — your boss? The one who runs Rising Voices? Nour, I'm not ready. I mean... what do I even say?  _(panicked, then catching himself)_
- **Nour:** You say what you just said to Faisal. Come on — she's by the window, and she's already looking this way.  _(encouraging, pulling him along)_
- **Macal:** Okay. Okay. One pitch. I've been working for this my whole life — I'm not running now.  _(breath in, resolve building, hopeful)_

**— COACH OUTRO (Mahmoud) —**

- **MAHMOUD (coach):** طَيِّب يا جَماعَة... إحْنا وَصَلْنا لآخِر المَشْهَدْ! النَّهارْدَه اتْعَلَّمْنا تَلاتَة عِبارات، تِفْتَح بِها أَي كَلامْ: "I couldn't help overhearing"... "Sorry to cut in"... وَ"I'll follow up myself." دُوُل مُفْتاحَك عَشان تِدْخُل عَلى أَي جَماعَة، وَتَوَرِّي إِنَّك صاحِب الفِكْرَة.  _(warm, wrapping up, steady rhythm)_
- **MAHMOUD (coach):** وَعَلى فِكْرَة... نُطْق /ŋ/ النَّهارْدَه كَمان! "talking"... "working"... "walking." الصَّوْت مِنْ وَرَا اللِّسَانْ، مَرَّة وَاحْدَة، مُش "talkin" وَمُش "walkeen."  _(playful emphasis, half-smile)_
- **MAHMOUD (coach):** وَدِلْوَأْتِي... عِبارَة الحَلْقَة. أَنا اخْتَرْت: "I'll follow up myself." لِيه؟ عَشان دِي عِبارَة المَسْؤُولِيَّة... بَتْقُول: أَنا هَعْمِل كِدَه بِنَفْسِي، مُش هَسْتَنَّى حَدْ. دِي عِبارَة تِخَلِّيك واقِف عَلى رِجْلِيك.  _(proud, clear, slow on the phrase)_
- **MAHMOUD (coach):** بَسْ أَنا عايِز أَسْمَعَك! سَجِّل فُويس نُوت صَغِير... قُول فِيه: "I'll follow up myself"... وَخَلِّي بالَك مِنْ نُطْقك، وَرِيّنا أَكْسَنْتِك. وَابْعَتْها عَلى تِلِجْرام... عَلى جَماعَة Empire English Community. إحْنا مُسْتَنيين سَمَاعَك، وَهَنِشَجَّعَك، أَصْلًا إِنْتَ مُش لَوَحْدَك فِي الحِكايَة!  _(inviting, encouraging, community warmth, big energy)_
