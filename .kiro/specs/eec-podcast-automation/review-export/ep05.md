## Episode 5 — “The Clause”  ·  B1  ·  ~5.6 min

**Situation:** Macals landlord Mr. Qureshi raises the rent and mentions visa paperwork.  
**Arc beat:** The 90-day clock becomes real.  
**Phonetic focus (Accent Lab):** Schwa /ə/ — The lazy, unstressed 'uh' vowel - the most common sound in English. 'about' -> 'uh-bout', 'sofa' -> 'sof-uh'. Unstressed syllables shrink to schwa.  
**Phrase of the episode:** *If you give me the time, I'll get it done.* — لَوْ إِدِّيتْنِي الْوَقْت، هَخَلَّصُه.  
**Cliffhanger:** Macals visa renewal depends on a full-time offer he doesnt have yet.


**— COLD OPEN —**

- **Landlord:** Macal, I'm not raising the rent because I want to. The building costs more now.  _(apologetic but firm, South Asian accent)_
- **Macal:** I understand. But if you raise it now, I might have to move. I've been here two years.  _(calm but tense, careful English)_
- **Landlord:** Two years, yes. But there's one more thing. Your visa paperwork — it needs renewing soon.  _(lowering voice, serious)_
- **Macal:** How soon?  _(sharp, anxious)_
- **Landlord:** Ninety days. If the papers aren't ready by then, I can't keep you here.  _(flat, final, no comfort)_

**— COACH INTRO (Mahmoud) —**

- **MAHMOUD (coach):** أَهْلًا يَا جَمَاعَة! أَنَا مَحْمُود، وَإِنْتَ مَعَ يَلَّا فْلُوَنْت... النَّهَارْدَه مَعَالْ بِيُوَاجِهْ مُشْكِلَة كَبِيرَة: الإِيجَارْ غَلِي، وَالْفِيزَا لَهَا تِسْعِين يَوْم... بَسْ!  _(warm, energetic welcome, building suspense)_
- **MAHMOUD (coach):** اسْمَعْ كَوَيِّس الْجُمْلَة دِي: I understand, but... — دِي بْتَفْتَحْ أَي مُفَاوَضَة بِأَدَب، مِشْ بِخُنَاقَة.  _(teaching emphasis, friendly)_
- **MAHMOUD (coach):** وَتَانِي جُمْلَة: if you raise it, I might... — لَاحِظْ إِنَّ 'مَايْتْ' مِشْ وَعْد، دِي احْتِمَال... وِالْاحْتِمَال بِيِدِّي مَجَالْ لِلْكَلَام.  _(slower, drawing attention to conditional)_
- **MAHMOUD (coach):** يَلَّا، نِسْمَعْ مَعَالْ بِيِتَفَاوَضْ إِزَّاي... وَخُدْ بَالَكْ مِنْ كَلِمَة by then — مَعْنَاهَا 'لِحَدّ سَاعِتْهَا'. يَلَّا!  _(playful, encouraging, ready to dive in)_

**— ACT 1 —**

- **Landlord:** Ninety days. If the papers aren't ready by then, I can't keep you here. I'm sorry, Macal. This is not personal.  _(firm but kind, matter-of-fact)_
- **Macal:** I know it's not personal. But this is a lot at once. The rent, and now the visa. Can I ask you something?  _(steady but tense, buying time)_
- **Landlord:** Of course. Ask.  _(open, patient)_
- **Macal:** If I sort out the visa paperwork myself, would you hold the old rent for three more months? I mean, just until I renew.  _(trying a conditional, careful)_
- **Landlord:** Hmm. Three months is a long time. But I like you, Macal. You pay on time, and you never make trouble.  _(weighing it, softening)_
- **Macal:** I live here since two years, and I've never missed a payment. If you give me the time, I'll get the papers done. I promise.  _(earnest, slightly pleading)_
- **Landlord:** Two months. Not three. And if the paperwork is late, we talk again. Fair?  _(final offer, no edge)_
- **Macal:** Fair. Two months. Thank you, Mr. Qureshi. I won't waste it.  _(relieved, grateful)_
- **Landlord:** Good. Start with the medical test. That one takes the longest. And Macal — don't wait until the last week.  _(practical, warning lightly)_
- **Macal:** I won't. I'll start tomorrow. Thanks again.  _(determined, exhaling)_
- **Landlord:** Inshallah it all works out. Goodnight, Macal.  _(warm, closing)_

**— COACH BREAK (Mahmoud) —**

- **MAHMOUD (coach):** طَيِّبْ يَا جَمَاعَة، نِشُوفْ مَعَالْ بِيِتَفَاوَضْ إِزَّاي... لَاحِظْ أَوَّلْ جُمْلَة: I know it's not personal, but... دِي بْتَعْتَرِفْ بِوَجْهَة نَظَرْ الطَّرَفْ التَّانِي، وِبَعْدِينْ بِتَحُطّْ طَلَبَكْ. يَعْنِي: I know it's not personal, but I need more time.  _(warm, teaching, steady pace)_
- **MAHMOUD (coach):** وَالْجُمْلَة التَّانْيَة: If you give me the time, I'll get the papers done. دِي شَرْطْ — الشَّرْطْ بِـ if، وِالنَّتِيجَة بِـ I'll. وِدِي أَقْوَى طَرِيقَة تِفَاوُضْ بِأَدَب: إِنْتَ تِدِّي، أَنَا أَعْمَلْ.  _(warm, emphasizing the pattern)_
- **MAHMOUD (coach):** وَخُدْ بَالَكْ: would you hold the old rent? — كَلِمَة would هِنَا بِتَخَلِّي الطَّلَبْ مُؤَدَّبْ، زِي مَا تِقُولْ 'يَا رِيتْ' بِالْمَصْرِي.  _(playful, inviting)_
- **MAHMOUD (coach):** ...بَسْ فِيهْ غَلْطَة مَعَالْ عَمَلْهَا: قَالْ I live here since two years. الْمَشْكِلَة إِنَّ كَلِمَة since بِتْيِيجِي مَعَ الْفِعْلْ فِي الْمَاضِي الْكَامِلْ: I have lived here since two years... لَأْ! بِالْأَمْرِيكِي الطَّبِيعِي: I have lived here for two years — بِـ for مِشْ since.  _(gentle correction, never shaming)_
- **MAHMOUD (coach):** تَعَالْ نِتْمَرَّنْ عَلَى صَوْتْ النَّهَارْدَه: Schwa — الْـ 'أَ' الْخَفِيفَة الْكَسْلَانَة. زِي فِي about: مِشْ a-bout، لَأْ — uh-bout. وِفِي الْمُشْكِلَة بْتَاعْتِنَا، كَلِمَة paper... الْمَقْطَعْ الثَّانِي بِيِخْتَفِي: pay-puh، مِشْ pay-per. يَلَّا كَرَّرْ مَعَايَا: about... paper... visa... beautiful!  _(playful, energetic drill, slow on examples)_

**— ACT 2 —**

- **Nour:** So? How did it go with Mr. Qureshi? You look like you just ran a marathon.  _(light, teasing, curious)_
- **Macal:** Almost. He agreed to hold the rent for two months. But only if I finish the visa paperwork before that. I've been here for two years, Nour, and now everything depends on ninety days.  _(relieved but tense, self-correcting)_
- **Nour:** Two months? That's actually good. You just need the medical test and the offer letter, right?  _(warm, practical)_
- **Macal:** The medical test, yes. But here's the problem. For the renewal, they need a full-time offer. Dune and Co. hasn't given me one yet. I'm still... contract to contract.  _(slow, dawning worry)_
- **Nour:** Wait. So you have the time now, but not the paper that actually keeps you here?  _(quiet, concerned)_
- **Macal:** Exactly. If I don't get that offer by then, the two months mean nothing. I bought time, Nour. I didn't buy my future.  _(steady, honest)_
- **Nour:** Then we go get it. The fellowship interview is in three weeks. If you win it, maybe that changes everything for Dune.  _(firm, encouraging)_
- **Macal:** If I win it. That's a big if. But you're right. I've lived here for two years. I'm not going to lose it sitting still.  _(rising, determined)_
- **Nour:** Good. Because I just checked your calendar. Your interview slot? They moved it up. It's Friday.  _(drop, half-apologetic, half-excited)_
- **Macal:** Friday? Nour, that's... that's four days from now. I haven't even prepared.  _(stunned, breath catching)_

**— COACH OUTRO (Mahmoud) —**

- **MAHMOUD (coach):** طَيِّبْ يَا جَمَاعَة، نِلَخِّصْ بِسُرْعَة... I know it's not personal, but I need more time — دِي تِفَاوُضْ بِأَدَبْ. If you give me the time, I'll get the papers done — شَرْطْ وِنَتِيجَة. وَ would you hold the old rent? — طَلَبْ مُؤَدَّبْ زِي 'يَا رِيتْ'. وَمَتْنَسَاشْ: I have lived here FOR two years، مِشْ since!  _(warm, quick recap, encouraging pace)_
- **MAHMOUD (coach):** وَعِبَارَة الْحَلَقَة النَّهَارْدَه هِيَّ... If you give me the time, I'll get it done. جُمْلَة وَاحْدَة، تِفْتَحْ بِهَا أَيِّ تَفَاوُضْ، مَعَ اللَانْدْلُورْدْ، مَعَ الْمُدِيرْ، مَعَ أَيِّ حَدْ!  _(building energy, proud, clear)_
- **MAHMOUD (coach):** دِلْوَقْتِي أَنَا عَايِزْ أَسْمَعْ صَوْتَكْ! سَجِّلْ فُويْسْ نُوتْ صُغَيَّرْ، قُولْ فِيهْ الْعِبَارَة بِصَوْتْ عَالِي وَوَاضِحْ، وَرَكِّزْ عَلَى الصَّوْتْ الْخَفِيفْ — about، paper، beautiful.  _(inviting, playful challenge)_
- **MAHMOUD (coach):** وَابْعَتُوا لِي عَلَى تِلِيجْرَامْ، فِي مَجْمُوعَة Empire English Community. إِحْنَا هِنِسْمَعْكُمْ، وِهِنسَجِّعْكُمْ وَاحِدْ وَاحِدْ... مِشْ وَاجِبْ، دِي عِيلَة!  _(belonging, affectionate, smiling)_
- **MAHMOUD (coach):** يَلَّا، مَعَالْ عِنْدُهْ أَرْبَعْ أَيَّامْ بَسْ... وِإِنْتَ كَمَانْ عِنْدَكْ فُرْصَة تِتْمَرَّنْ النَّهَارْدَه. أَشُوفْكُمْ فِي الْحَلَقَة الْجَايَة. يَلَّا فْلُوِنْتْ!  _(warm sign-off, motivating, upbeat)_
