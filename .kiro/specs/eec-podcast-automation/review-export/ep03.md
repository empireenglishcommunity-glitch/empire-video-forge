## Episode 3 — “The Coffee Order”  ·  A2+  ·  ~6.2 min

**Situation:** Macal buys coffee for Nour at Common Grounds; Aisha the barista helps him.  
**Arc beat:** First real connection with Nour. He realizes he has been imitating British English.  
**Phonetic focus (Accent Lab):** TH sounds — Two THs: voiceless in 'think/three' and voiced in 'this/that'. Tongue lightly between the teeth - not 's', not 'z', not 't'.  
**Phrase of the episode:** *I have lived here for two years* — أَنَا عَايِش هِنَا مِنْ سَنِتَيْن  
**Cliffhanger:** Nour: Im from Michigan, not London. Tarek posts a polished Rising Voices video.


**— COLD OPEN —**

- **Barista:** Next, please! What can I get for you today?  _(brisk, friendly, cafe rush)_
- **Macal:** Yes... one coffee, please. For my friend. She is American...  _(nervous, halting, phone in hand)_
- **Barista:** Of course. What kind? Latte? Americano?  _(patient, warm)_
- **Macal:** I... I am not sure. She likes it sweet, but not too sweet.  _(thinking hard, quiet panic)_
- **Barista:** No problem. We have a caramel latte. It is sweet, but not too sweet.  _(reassuring, clear)_
- **Macal:** Okay. Yes. One caramel latte. And... can I ask your name?  _(relieved, then bold)_
- **Barista:** Aisha. And yours?  _(smiling, curious)_
- **Macal:** Macal. Nice to meet you, Aisha. This is my first time ordering alone.  _(honest, slightly proud)_

**— COACH INTRO (Mahmoud) —**

- **MAHMOUD (coach):** أَهْلًا بِيكُمْ يا جَمَاعَة فِي يَلَّا فْلُوئِنْت! أَنَا مَحْمُود... الكُوتشْ بِتَاعْكُمْ، وَالنَّهَارْدَهْ مَعَانَا مَشْهَدْ حِلْو جِدًّا.  _(warm, welcoming, upbeat)_
- **MAHMOUD (coach):** مَاكَالْ رَايِحْ يِشْتَرِي قَهْوَة لِنُور... أَوَّلْ مَرَّة يِطْلُبْ لَوَحْدُهْ فِي دُبَايْ! وَالْبَارِيسْتَا آيشَا بِتَسَاعْدُهْ.  _(playful, storytelling energy)_
- **MAHMOUD (coach):** اسْمَعُوا كُوَيِّسْ عَلَى تَلَاتَة تَعْبِيرَاتْ: الأُولَى... What can I get for you today? التَّانْيَة... I am not sure. وَالتَّالْتَة... sweet, but not too sweet.  _(clear, enunciating target phrases slowly)_
- **MAHMOUD (coach):** وَبُصُّوا كَمَانْ... مَاكَالْ لِسَّهْ بِيِقَلِّدْ الإِنْجْلِيزِي الْبِرِيطَانِي مِنْ غِيرْ مَا يِحِسْ. يَلَّا نِسْمَعْ المَشْهَدْ مَعَ بَعْضْ!  _(conspiratorial, then excited)_

**— ACT 1 —**

- **Barista:** Here you go, Macal. One caramel latte. Are you sure that is all? Your friend, she is waiting outside?  _(warm, friendly, slightly teasing)_
- **Macal:** Yes... but no. She is not waiting. I will meet her at the office. Nour is... she is my colleague. And maybe my friend. I do not know yet.  _(earnest, a little nervous, slower rhythm)_
- **Barista:** You like her. It is okay. So what does she drink usually? Do you know her order?  _(knowing smile, gentle)_
- **Macal:** I am not sure. She always orders something... but I do not remember the name. It is brown, and it has milk.  _(searching for words, honest)_
- **Barista:** A mocha? Or maybe a flat white? Do not worry. We can fix this. Is she a sweet person?  _(helpful, patient, light humour)_
- **Macal:** Yes, she is very sweet. But the coffee... sweet, but not too sweet. Does that make sense?  _(earnest, thinking out loud)_
- **Barista:** Perfect sense. So, one caramel latte for her. And for you? It is your first time here alone, yes? Try something new.  _(encouraging, brisk but warm)_
- **Macal:** Okay. I will take an Americano. Black, please. No sugar. I need to stay awake. I live here since two years, but this city... it never sleeps.  _(relaxing slightly, then earnest)_
- **Barista:** Two coffees coming up. That will be thirty-five dirhams. You are doing great, by the way. Ordering alone is not easy.  _(kind, matter-of-fact)_
- **Macal:** Thank you, Aisha. Really. Yesterday I asked for a "cup of tea" and everyone was laughing. I think I sound like... a British man?  _(self-deprecating, curious)_
- **Barista:** A little. But that is okay. You are learning. Here is your change, and your coffees. Enjoy, Macal.  _(reassuring, brisk)_
- **Macal:** Wait. Can I ask one more thing? When I give this to Nour... what should I say? Something natural. Not too... formal.  _(hesitant, hopeful)_
- **Barista:** Just say, "I got you a caramel latte. I hope it is sweet, but not too sweet." Trust me. She will smile.  _(playful, encouraging)_

**— COACH BREAK (Mahmoud) —**

- **MAHMOUD (coach):** يَلَّا نِفَكِّكْ تَعْبِيرَاتْ المَشْهَدْ... أُولَ حَاجَةْ: What can I get for you today? يَعْنِي إِيهْ؟ الْبَارِيسْتَا بِتَسْأَلْكَ عَايِزْ تِشْرَبْ إِيهْ. تِسْمَعْهَا كَتِيرْ فِي الْكَافِيهَاتْ. مَثَلًا... Good morning! What can I get for you today?  _(warm, teaching, clear)_
- **MAHMOUD (coach):** تَانِي حَاجَةْ: I am not sure. يَعْنِي... أَنَا مُشْ مُتَأَكِّدْ. لَمَّا مِشْ عَارِفْ الإِجَابَة، قُولْهَا بِهُدُوءْ. مَثَلًا... I am not sure, can you help me? وِتْلَاتَةْ: sweet, but not too sweet... حِلْوَة، بَسْ مِشْ أَوِي. تِقُولْهَا لَمَّا تِوَصْفْ حَاجَة.  _(encouraging, step-by-step)_
- **MAHMOUD (coach):** وَدَلْوَقْتِي... غَلْطَةْ مَاكَالْ. قَالْ: I live here since two years. الْغَلْطَةْ... كَلِمَةْ since مَعَ الْمُضَارِعْ الْمُسْتَمِرْ، مُشْ مَعَ الْمُضَارِعْ الْبَسِيطْ. فِي الْعَرَبِي بِنْقُولْ "بَاعِيشْ هِنا مِنْ سَنِتَيْن" — فَبِنْنَقْلْهَا زِي مَا هِيَّ. الصَّحْ... I have lived here for two years. زَمَنْ for، مُشْ since!  _(gentle, correcting, not shaming)_
- **MAHMOUD (coach):** يَلَّا نِلْعَبْ! الْمَعْمَلْ بَتَاعْ النُّطْقْ... صَوْتْ TH. فِي صَوْتَيْنْ: وَاحِدْ نَاعِمْ زِي think, ووَاحِدْ مَجْهُورْ زِي this. اللِّسَانْ بِيِطْلَعْ شُوَيَّةْ بَيْنْ السِّنَانْ... مُشْ س، مُشْ ز، مُشْ ت.  _(playful, inviting, high energy)_
- **MAHMOUD (coach):** مِنْ الْمَشْهَدْ... كَلِمَةْ three. لَوْ قُلْتْها تري بِالتَّاءْ... غَلَطْ. الصَّحْ... three. كَرِّرْ بَعْدِي: three! وَكَمَانْ كَلِمَةْ that. مُشْ زاتْ وَلَا داتْ. الصَّحْ... that. كَرِّرْ: that! that! عَاشْ! أَنْتَ بِتِحْسِنْ... وَاحِدَةْ وَاحِدَةْ.  _(playful drill, celebratory end)_

**— ACT 2 —**

- **Barista:** Here is your change, Macal. And remember — sweet, but not too sweet.  _(warm, playful reminder)_
- **Macal:** Thank you, Aisha. I will try. I have lived here for two years, but I am still learning... how to speak like myself.  _(earnest, small laugh at himself)_
- **Nour:** Macal! You got coffee? And it is... wait. Is this a caramel latte?  _(surprised, genuinely pleased)_
- **Macal:** Yes. I got you a caramel latte. I hope it is sweet, but not too sweet.  _(careful, proud of the line)_
- **Nour:** That is exactly what I always order. How did you know? This is so nice. Okay, now I have to tell you something.  _(delighted, leaning in)_
- **Macal:** Something good? Or something bad? Your face is... I do not know. It is doing two things.  _(nervous, trying to joke)_
- **Nour:** Not bad. I just heard Tarek is posting his Rising Voices video today. And listen — this accent I have? People think it is British. It is not. I am from Michigan, not London.  _(casual, then firm and honest)_
- **Macal:** Michigan... not London. So when I copy a British accent, I am copying nobody in the room.  _(slow realization, quiet)_
- **Nour:** Nobody but you, Macal. Hey — where are you going? Your coffee is still hot!  _(calling after him, confused smile)_
- **Macal:** Tarek's video. If he posts it today, I cannot wait for tomorrow. I have to make my own video... today. Right now.  _(sudden urgency, determined)_

**— COACH OUTRO (Mahmoud) —**

- **MAHMOUD (coach):** يَلَّا، نِلَخِّصْ بِسُرْعَةْ... النَّهَارْدَةْ اتْعَلَّمْنَا: What can I get for you today? يَعْنِي عَايِزْ تِشْرَبْ إِيهْ. وَ I am not sure... أَنَا مُشْ مُتَأَكِّدْ. وَ sweet, but not too sweet... حِلْوَة، بَسْ مِشْ أَوِي. وَأَهَمْ وَاحِدَةْ... I have lived here for two years. زَمَنْ for، مُشْ since!  _(recapping fast and clear, ticking phrases off like a checklist)_
- **MAHMOUD (coach):** وَعِبَارَةْ الْحَلْقَةْ النَّهَارْدَةْ هِيَّ... I have lived here for two years. يَلَّا، قُولْهَا مَعَايَا: I have lived here for two years! بِصَوْتْكْ أَنْتَ... مُشْ صَوْتِي أَنَا.  _(proud, slows down for the phrase, then playful challenge)_
- **MAHMOUD (coach):** وَدَلْوَقْتِي، التَّحَدِّي بَتَاعْنَا... سَجِّلْ فُويْسْنُوتْ صَغِيرْ، فِيهْ تِقُولْ الْعِبَارَةْ دِي بِنَفْسْ النُّطْقْ بَتَاعْ النَّهَارْدَةْ... I have lived here for two years. وَابْعَتْهَا لِـ Empire English Community عَلَى تِلِيجْرَامْ.  _(warm, inviting, building excitement for the challenge)_
- **MAHMOUD (coach):** إِحْنَا هِنِسْمَعْ صَوْتْكْ، وَنِشَجِّعْكْ... وَاحِدَةْ وَاحِدَةْ. مُشْ وَاجِبْ، دَهْ بَيْتْ. اسْتَعْمِلْ صَوْتْ TH الصَّحِيحْ، وَخَلِّينَا نِسْمَعْكْ!  _(warm, belonging tone, ends with bright energy)_
- **MAHMOUD (coach):** أَنَا مَحْمُودْ... وَأَنْتَ مُشْ لِوَحْدَكْ. يَلَّا نِتْكَلَّمْ... يَلَّا فْلُوَنْتْ!  _(signing off with warmth and a smile)_
