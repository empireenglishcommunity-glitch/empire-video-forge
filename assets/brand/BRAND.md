# Empire English Community — Brand Assets

## Logo
- `eec-logo.jpg` — 800×800, official channel emblem (from the YouTube channel
  "EEC | MACAL EMPIRE").
- **Style:** circular heraldic emblem, metallic gold on deep black.
- **Elements:** crown → shield with stylized "E" (sound-wave motif) → laurel
  wreaths → equalizer sound-wave bars (left/right) → circular text
  "EMPIRE ENGLISH COMMUNITY" / "EMPIRE — ENGLISH COMMUNITY — EEC".

## Palette (locked)
- **Primary gold:** metallic gold, approx `#D4AF37` / `#F5C518` highlights.
- **Background:** deep black `#000000` / near-black `#0A0A0A`.
- (Earlier "navy" idea is replaced by **gold-on-black** to match the logo exactly.)

## Usage in the editing pipeline
- **Intro sting (animated):** logo fades/scales in on black; the built-in
  equalizer sound-wave bars animate/pulse; gold shimmer sweep; ~2–3s. Outro:
  logo + subscribe CTA, ~2s.
- **Thumbnails:** gold text on high-contrast background; may place a small logo
  lockup corner badge for consistency.
- **Captions (Shorts):** current-word highlight uses the brand gold.

## Rules
- Do not recolor the logo; keep gold-on-black.
- Keep clear space around the emblem (≥10% of its width) in any composite.
